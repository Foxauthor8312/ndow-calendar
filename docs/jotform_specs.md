Overview

The NDOW Volunteer Portal integrates with Jotform to collect post-event participant feedback.

Survey emails are generated by the Communications module and contain personalized links with hidden metadata fields.

Upon submission, Jotform performs an HTTP POST directly to the NDOW backend, allowing survey responses to be processed in real time without polling or scheduled synchronization.

I would even add a note in the manual describing the experiment:

Proof of Concept: During development, a Jotform survey was configured with hidden fields (event_id, customer_id, student_email, etc.) and Redirect with HTTP POST enabled. Testing confirmed that both the visible survey responses and the hidden metadata were successfully delivered to the NDOW backend, validating the architecture for future implementation.

That's the kind of engineering note that explains why the system was designed the way it was.
