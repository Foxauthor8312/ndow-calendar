Independent Platform Notice

This platform is independently developed and is not an official Nevada Department of Wildlife (NDOW) product, service, or information system.

The system is intended solely as a volunteer-operated coordination and event visibility tool utilizing publicly displayed event information.

The platform does not interfere with, modify, access, bypass, or invade any portion of the official NDOW web application or operational infrastructure.
